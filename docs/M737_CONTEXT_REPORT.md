## Root causes and corrections to the audit interpretation

Essentials could clip accepted output yet save the complete input endpoint and fingerprint. A second oversized tightening could also be accepted. Completion now means usable structural output for the processed source entries, never verified retention of every fact. Source changes, writer corrections, changed premise, manual edits and timeline ownership prevent stale replacement.

The semantic recall index omitted separate detail and relied on the opening 220 characters. Its independent recent slice could exclude entries the actual hybrid did not carry. Assembly also reconstructed entry membership with substring checks on rendered lines. Selection now uses IDs and whole objects first. Literal cues are source excerpts rather than new facts, with helper room calculated from its effective provider allowance. Timeout, malformed output, cancellation and disabled behavior preserve local recall.

Plans used stored order alone. Selection now weighs the move, scene, participants, current concerns, goals and condition text. Matching a condition makes a plan relevant, never established as satisfied. Conditions, exact words, completed steps and disposition remain whole; other plans stay stored.

Knowledge views and legacy broadcasting could treat public sounding words or presence as acquisition evidence. Missing books could produce categorical ignorance, and overlap could merge belief, negation or conditional statements. Both the storage operations and application generated views now preserve these distinctions. A private fact naming Ivo does not give Ivo awareness. A learned truth can replace the corresponding belief for its actual recipient, while suspicion cannot. Explicit witness decisions remain supported. Ordinary knowledge and reasonable inference do not require invented lessons.

Final fitting formerly removed dialogue without a final source account. Fitting now recomputes the final manifest and perspective view, keeps the newest exchange and writer instructions, reports unrepresented source intervals and uses the same serializer for output reservation. Required content still raises the clear size error if it cannot fit. Source representation is distinguished from semantic retention.
