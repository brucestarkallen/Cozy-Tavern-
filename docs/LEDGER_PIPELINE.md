## Important distinctions

People core holds role, rank, family, background and established enduring
nature. What's true holds durable appearance, with separate keys for explicitly
established alternate faces. Scene holds ending pose and clothes. Elsewhere
holds World decisions for absent people. Arcs and standings retain earned
interpretation; restoring a missing source fact is not permission to reset them.
A quiet person is not absent; an unnamed individually established sibling is
not a generic crowd extra; a design comparison is not another cast member.

Prompt limits are view limits. A record not shown is indexed for lookup and
must be fetched before its unshown field is replaced. Source facts already
read are reused with original quotations and matching source marks. New turns
still use the existing worker calls and optional fetches; M713 adds no extra
model or check before storytelling. Existing malformed-output, missing-world-
decision and transport retries remain.

Durable open threads no longer disappear at40, and learned knowledge no longer
evicts facts at60. Actual closures, truth replacing belief, retirement and
other existing story operations still apply. Legacy knowledge-loss horizons
remain conservative because M713 cannot recover already-deleted facts merely
by enlarging current storage. Original-source retrieval supplies recovery.
Field bounds remain: People core/state/arc4000 characters, individual physical
facts1000 and keys120. Founder cards/lore and prompt room also retain existing
limits. This milestone does not claim unlimited context or infallible model
interpretation, and does not silently raise every context budget.

## M718 source identity handoff

Original source references retain literal text and stable section marks. A
contextual description is not another cast member merely because it is a noun
phrase. Source reading receives existing identity hints but originals govern.
Final audit persists its source-local identity decision with identities or
source.resolve, linking the extracted mention to its actual person or actual
nonperson/reference kind. Same descriptors on different pages can refer to
different people. Source lookup and identity/appearance inventory honor the
same row-local binding; no global descriptor alias or cross-page shortcut.
Missing decisions are completed in the current review conversation. Original
receipt coverage and valid unchanged entry approvals stay reusable. Source
metadata corrections are journaled and undoable, with completion invalidated
on undo. No additional worker, guard model or mandatory source rescan.

## M737 request views and perspective boundaries

Stored records remain durable. Request selection uses complete entry objects,
attached detail, source identity and visible versions, with a final manifest of
original, folded, compressed and unrepresented stretches. Essentials completion
is structural source processing, not a certificate that every fact survived.
Unusable candidates retain prior usable text and pending source material.
Knowledge recipients belong to explicit source readings. Presence, a name in a
secret and public sounding words do not supply acquisition evidence. Legacy
missing books express uncertainty, never certain ignorance. Exact dates,
certainty, negation, conditions and optional pathways remain distinct through
knowledge writes, views and undo. Actual selected perspective evidence can
remove a duplicate retelling while preserving another person’s belief.
Existing factual audit authority and repair policy remain unchanged.


## M738 perspective consistency correction

Knowledge views and belief updates share the same interpretation of optional
kind and clearly supported legacy prefixes. Exact duplicate equivalence is
separate from learning an explicit belief's correcting truth. Conflicting
metadata or uncertain source ownership never authorizes suppression or belief
replacement. The actual recipient's knowledge operation may supersede a belief
with the matching untrue relationship; rumor, suspicion and ignorance may not.
Undo retains the prior full record and journal replay keeps recipient ownership.
Final selected original evidence carries its existing message identity, version
and told page date. Quotes alone, unknown chronology and transformed source
copies cannot certify current perspective. Legacy records stay stored; no bulk
rewrite, migration, additional reader or audit policy change is required.


## M743 completion and recovery

A source fact finding retains sourceId and sourceName independently of the
canonical People key. Missing legacy labels recover only from a unique matching
fact in a current original receipt with an established row binding. Semantic
completion is scoped to the actual source mark, facts, binding and saved profile;
uncertainty stays open and completed unchanged reviews generate no new calls.
Repair policy9 resumes previously paused failures once. New factual work and
saved closures refresh progress; two repeated stalled runs remain bounded.
Essentials retains the same 16000 character budget, existing source ownership
and previous usable text. Oversized sections divide chronologically after the
existing draft revisions; complete leaf receipts survive reload and only the
remaining sections are retried. Structural coverage is not a semantic guarantee.
