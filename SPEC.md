# The open milestone

No milestone is open.

When the owner asks for work that spans more than one session or more than one agent,
write its plan here before building, and keep it current while the work is open:

- His request, in his words, with the date.
- What it touches: the modules, and the contracts in AGENTS.md it must keep.
- What done means, in behaviour he would see.
- The checks that will prove it (AGENTS.md, Testing: real checks only).

When the milestone is published, its record goes to HANDOFF.md and HISTORY.md and this
file returns to "No milestone is open". Earlier specs are archived verbatim in
docs/archive (SPEC.before-M752-tidy.md holds the specs of M684 to M738; it was not
kept current after M738).
